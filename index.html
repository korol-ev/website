Our Story

We Build Power That Doesn't Ask Permission.

Koral exists because energy access shouldn't be a function of geography, infrastructure budgets or utility timelines. We engineer systems that generate, store and deliver power wherever it's needed.

Mission

Decentralize the Grid. Permanently.

Centralised energy infrastructure was designed for a different century. It's expensive to extend, slow to permit and fragile under stress. Our mission is to make autonomous clean energy the default choice — not the backup plan — for fleets, industry and emergency services.

We do this by building the entire stack ourselves: solar harvesting, storage chemistry, power electronics and the software that orchestrates all of it.
